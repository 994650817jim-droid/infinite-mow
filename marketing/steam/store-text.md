## 中文

### 一句话简介（≤300 字符）

以三国为舞台的水墨割草动作游戏。率领主公与部曲，在千军万马中杀出血路：斩首领、劝降将，攻城拔寨，在天下地图上把一座座城池收入版图，从黄巾之乱一路打到北伐中原。

### 详细介绍

**东汉末年，天下大乱。**
你是一支义军的主公。从黄巾之乱起兵，经讨伐董卓、群雄割据、官渡、赤壁、入蜀、汉中、襄樊、夷陵、南征，直到北伐——十二章史事，一关一关打下来。

**割草，但是三国的割草**
- 每一关都是满屏敌军。主将自动出招，你负责走位、拾取、在升级时挑选战法，把一套构筑越滚越大。
- 25 种战法分属 8 个流派（连击、暴击、游击、冲锋、烈焰、控场、统御、守御），凑齐流派还能觉醒 12 种流派奥义。
- 不是一个人在打：刀盾、长枪、弓弩、骑兵等 8 类部曲随你出征，主将阵亡、部曲溃散都会改变战局。

**名将与首领**
- 12 位可玩武将：赵云、关羽、张飞、刘备、诸葛亮、马超、黄忠、魏延、庞统、黄月英、孙尚香、貂蝉，各有本命兵器、专属武艺和被动。
- 34 名关底首领，招式各不相同：骑马冲阵的华雄、二阶段下马狂暴的吕布、颜良文丑双首领同场……
- 把首领打到残血，可以选择**劝降、斩杀，或再压一压**——劝降成功的武将会加入你的麾下。

**攻城与天下**
- 攻城不是普通关卡：拔帅旗、毁箭楼、护送冲车撞门、破瓮城、最后占领府衙。小、中、大三档城池打法各不相同。
- 天下地图上有 31 座城池、15 路诸侯。攻下的城会归入你的版图，疆域随之扩张，战争迷雾像墨迹在宣纸上退去；边城还可能告急，需要回援。
- 攻下的城池可以建设、每战产粮；军情事件（护送商队、清剿流寇、悬赏敌将）随季节刷新。

**营寨经营**
- 从一座小营寨起步，扩建为县城、州府：军营、农田、讲武堂、招贤馆、马厩、粮仓、麒麟阁……
- 讲武堂研究 13 项科技、军营练兵、招贤馆招募随从、马厩换乘 11 匹名马（赤兔、的卢、绝影……），全都用打仗挣来的军粮。

**水墨与书法**
- 手绘水墨开场、书法字幕、剧情卷轴：每一章都有史事对白与立绘。
- 战斗中全部特效都是手绘序列帧，不是程序画的像素粒子。
- 配乐为原创的五声音阶战鼓与笛箫，首领战另有专属旋律。

## English

### Short description (≤300 characters)

A hand-painted, ink-wash horde-slaying action game set in China's Three Kingdoms. Lead your lord and troops through endless armies, duel legendary generals, persuade them to defect, storm walled cities and paint the realm in your colours — from the Yellow Turbans to the Northern Campaigns.

### About This Game

**The Han dynasty is falling. The land is at war.**
You command a band of loyalist volunteers. Rise up during the Yellow Turban Rebellion and fight your way through twelve chapters of history — Dong Zhuo, the warlord era, Guandu, Red Cliffs, the conquest of Shu, Hanzhong, Fancheng, Yiling, the Southern Campaign and finally the Northern Expeditions.

**Horde slaying, Three Kingdoms style**
- Every stage floods the screen with enemies. Your general attacks automatically; you move, collect, and choose tactics on level-up to snowball a build.
- 25 tactics across 8 schools (Combo, Critical, Skirmish, Charge, Flame, Control, Command, Bulwark). Complete a school to awaken one of 12 school techniques.
- You never fight alone: 8 kinds of troops — sword-and-shield, spear, bow, crossbow, cavalry and more — march with you, and how they hold up shapes the battle.

**Heroes and bosses**
- 12 playable heroes: Zhao Yun, Guan Yu, Zhang Fei, Liu Bei, Zhuge Liang, Ma Chao, Huang Zhong, Wei Yan, Pang Tong, Huang Yueying, Sun Shangxiang and Diaochan — each with a signature weapon, martial arts and passives.
- 34 stage bosses with their own movesets: Hua Xiong charging on horseback, Lü Bu dismounting into a berserk second phase, Yan Liang and Wen Chou fighting side by side…
- Bring a boss to the brink and choose: **persuade, execute, or press harder**. Generals who yield join your ranks.

**Sieges and the realm**
- Sieges are their own kind of battle: tear down banners, burn arrow towers, escort the battering ram to the gate, break the barbican and seize the governor's hall. Small, medium and large cities each play differently.
- The world map holds 31 cities and 15 warlords. Every city you take joins your realm, your borders grow, and the fog of war recedes like ink on rice paper — while border towns may come under attack and call for relief.
- Captured cities can be developed and feed your army after every battle; seasonal intel events (escort a caravan, clear out bandits, hunt a wanted general) keep the map moving.

**Build your camp**
- Grow a small war camp into a county seat and then a provincial capital: barracks, farms, an academy, a recruiting hall, stables, granaries and the Qilin Pavilion.
- Research 13 technologies, drill your troops, recruit followers and ride 11 famous horses — Red Hare, Dilu, Shadow Runner and more — all paid for with the grain you win in battle.

**Ink and calligraphy**
- A hand-painted ink-wash opening, calligraphy captions and story scrolls with dialogue and portraits for every chapter.
- Every combat effect is a hand-painted animation, not procedural pixel particles.
- An original pentatonic score of war drums, flutes and zithers, with dedicated themes for boss fights.

### Feature list (short form)

- 12 historical chapters, 36 stages — from the Yellow Turbans to the Northern Expeditions
- 12 playable heroes with signature weapons and unique arts
- 34 distinct bosses: mounted bosses, twin-boss fights, berserk second phases
- Persuade defeated bosses to join you
- Three tiers of siege battles: battering rams, drawbridges, arrow towers, barbicans
- World map: 31 cities, 15 warlords, fog of war, border alerts and relief battles
- 8 troop types fighting at your side
- 25 tactics · 8 schools · 12 awakenings
- Camp building: 8 buildings, 13 technologies, 11 famous horses
- Hand-painted ink-wash art and calligraphy
